## Purpose

Examples illustrate how DesignLogic Semantic Runtime Architecture may represent an intent-to-execution flow through structured meaning, bounded context, review boundaries, handoff artifacts, and authority boundaries.

They are architecture illustrations only. An example is not implementation proof, evidence of an operating semantic backend, a validated product workflow, an authorization, or a deployment artifact.

## Relationship To The Architecture

An example may:

- show how raw intent can be interpreted into structured meaning units;
- make assumptions and open questions visible;
- identify review and authority boundaries;
- sketch a bounded execution context or handoff artifact; and
- demonstrate why trace and refinement remain separate from proof or approval.

It does not establish that DesignLogic Framework modules or DesignLogic Apps implement the illustrated pattern.
